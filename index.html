Loop™ Guide

How Loop works

Loop is built to make everyday decisions easier by organizing useful recommendations around your tastes, your context, real experiences, and the people and communities most relevant to you.

Local discoveryTrusted recommendationsAlpha
1

Search what you need

Look for a place, service, product category, or everyday need near you — from restaurants and auto care to home services and more.

2

Start with your current Loop

Loop is designed to prioritize useful experiences from people connected to your local area and the communities you trust, then expand outward when more context is helpful.

3

Understand why it fits

Recommendations are meant to be supported by firsthand experiences, recency, community agreement, and contributor reputation — not just a generic star average.

4

Add your experience

When you visit, buy, hire, or use something, share what actually happened. Your experience can help someone else in your Loop make a better decision.

What is a “Loop”?

Your Loop is the community context around you — the people, places, routines, and local knowledge that are most relevant to your everyday life. As Loop grows, you will be able to use your current Loop, a home Loop, or explore outside your Loop when you want broader recommendations.

Alpha note: Loop is still being built. During Alpha, some places may appear as New while community experiences and Loop Ratings are still being gathered. The long-term experience is designed to rank and explain recommendations using trusted local signals.

Community Guidelines
Loop™ Community

Community Guidelines

Loop works best when people can share real experiences, ask questions, disagree respectfully, and participate without being harassed, excluded, or manipulated.

RespectSafetyAuthenticity
1

Be respectful

Keep conversations civil and focused on the experience. Criticism is welcome; personal attacks, humiliation, threats, intimidation, and bullying are not.

2

Everyone belongs

Loop supports equal participation regardless of gender, gender identity, race, ethnicity, religion, disability, age, sexual orientation, nationality, or other protected characteristics. Hate and discriminatory harassment are not acceptable.

3

Share what you actually know

Recommendations and reviews should reflect genuine firsthand experience whenever possible. Do not fabricate visits, purchases, results, credentials, or relationships.

4

Protect privacy

Do not post private addresses, phone numbers, financial information, medical details, passwords, or other sensitive personal information about another person without a legitimate reason and appropriate permission.

5

No manipulation or spam

Do not coordinate fake ratings, create deceptive accounts, repeatedly post irrelevant promotions, impersonate others, or offer incentives for dishonest recommendations.

6

Keep it useful

Focus on information that helps someone make a decision: what happened, what stood out, when it happened, and who the place, product, or service may be a good fit for.

Disagreement is okay. Abuse is not.

Two people can have completely different experiences with the same place or service. Loop is designed to make room for those differences. The expectation is simple: challenge the recommendation if needed, not the dignity of the person who shared it.

Moderation in Alpha: Reporting, review, enforcement, and appeals tools will continue to expand as Loop grows. Content or behavior that threatens safety, targets people with harassment, or deliberately manipulates community trust may be limited or removed as those systems come online.

Trust & Safety

Loop is designed around useful firsthand experience, not anonymous popularity. These principles describe how the Alpha is intended to protect recommendation quality and community trust.

Alpha principlesEvolving with testing
1

Firsthand over hearsay

Experiences should come from people who actually visited, purchased, hired, used, or meaningfully interacted with the place or service they are describing.

2

Reputation is earned

Loop Reputation is intended to reflect useful evidence, consistency, recency, and category experience—not follower counts or popularity alone.

3

No rating manipulation

Fabricated experiences, coordinated voting, impersonation, spam, incentives for dishonest ratings, and attempts to game Loop signals are not acceptable.

4

Respect and accountability

Contributions should be specific, relevant, and respectful. Reporting and moderation tools will continue to expand as the Alpha grows.

Alpha note: Trust and moderation systems are still being built. Loop may revise these principles as reporting, verification, business claims, and reputation features develop.

Privacy Policy
Loop™ Alpha

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the current Loop Alpha experience handles information when you use location-based search, create a profile, save places, react to experiences, contribute content, or use Loop Mini Games and leaderboards.

Last updated October 7, 2026Interim Alpha policy
Alpha transparency: Loop is still being built. Secure Alpha accounts and selected centralized features are now connected, while some information still remains only in your browser on your device. Third-party infrastructure may process account, technical, location-related, feedback, and leaderboard information needed to provide the experience.

1. Information Loop may use

Location information

When you choose to search nearby, Loop asks your browser for permission to access your device location. If you allow it, Loop uses that location to define the nearby search area and request relevant place results. You can deny or revoke location permission through your browser or device settings.

Profile and preference information

The Alpha can store profile details you enter, saved places, reactions, recent contribution information, and similar preferences locally in your browser using device storage. Clearing site data or browser storage may remove this information.

Content you contribute

If you submit an experience, rating, review, or other contribution, Loop may use that content to provide the feature, show it back to you, calculate product signals, and—when community publishing is enabled—display it to other users subject to these policies.

Arcade and leaderboard information

Loop Mini Games can keep a device high score in your browser. If you are signed in, Loop may centrally store your best score for a game in a particular Loop together with your account identifier, display name, and a coarse Loop label used for the leaderboard. Public leaderboard display is shortened to your first name and last initial. Guests can play the games without posting centralized leaderboard scores.

Feedback and support information

When you use Send feedback, Loop may centrally store the feedback type, message, optional follow-up email you choose to provide, the page where feedback was submitted, basic browser information, and a submission timestamp. This information is used to review Alpha feedback, troubleshoot issues, improve Loop, and contact you if you request or permit follow-up.

Technical information

Hosting, map, place-search, and network providers may receive standard technical information such as IP address, browser details, request metadata, and timestamps when your device communicates with their services.

2. How information is used

  • Provide nearby place discovery and Loop search results.
  • Remember your Alpha preferences, saved places, profile details, reactions, contributions, and device game bests.
  • Operate Loop Mini Games leaderboards and compare signed-in members' best scores within a current Loop or across Loops.
  • Develop, test, secure, troubleshoot, and improve Loop, including reviewing Alpha feedback and notifying the project team when new feedback is submitted.
  • Prevent misuse, fraud, manipulation of ratings or leaderboards, or abuse of community features.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations when required.

Loop Guide preferences

Members can select interests by category. Those choices are stored in their private profile; optional activity learning uses device storage and can be turned off and cleared in the preference editor. Guests do not have a saved personalized profile. Preferences help rank listings with supporting category or name evidence, but they do not establish that a place has been reviewed, independently owned, or verified to offer particular items or amenities. Loop Guide does not automatically change deployed app code.

3. Location controls

Loop does not require location access for every feature. Nearby search starts with at least a 30-mile Loop and expands outward in 10-mile steps when you choose to widen it. Nearby search and the optional current-Loop arcade leaderboard can use location permission, and your browser or device controls whether that permission is granted. If location is unavailable for the arcade, Loop can fall back to your saved Home Loop or a general leaderboard context.

Location-related coordinates may be sent to an OpenStreetMap-related service to resolve nearby places or identify a coarse current Loop. The arcade leaderboard stores the resulting Loop label/key with a score rather than storing the latitude and longitude in the arcade score record. Loop should not be used to infer that a location result is perfectly precise or continuously tracked.

4. Third-party services

The current Alpha uses third-party infrastructure to operate. This includes Vercel for hosting and server functions; OpenStreetMap-related services, including Nominatim, for map imagery, nearby place discovery, and coarse current-Loop resolution; Wikimedia services, including Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons, when a place has a verified public photo reference available; Supabase for secure Alpha authentication, profiles, centralized feedback, and arcade leaderboard scores; and Resend for feedback email notifications. These providers may process information under their own terms and privacy practices.

As Loop adds analytics, authentication, payments, advertising, or additional integrations, this policy should be updated before those systems begin collecting information materially beyond the current Alpha behavior.

5. Selling information and advertising

The current Loop Alpha does not operate a system for selling users' personal information and does not currently use behavioral advertising based on your activity in the Alpha. If Loop later introduces advertising or data practices that materially change this statement, this policy will be updated and any required choices or notices will be provided.

6. Retention and deletion

Information stored locally in your browser remains there until it is removed by you, your browser, or the Alpha experience. Account/profile information, centralized feedback, and signed-in arcade leaderboard bests may also be stored in Loop's connected backend. Third-party providers may keep technical logs according to their own retention practices. Account-level retention and deletion controls should be finalized and documented before general availability.

7. Children

Loop Alpha is not directed to children under 13, and children under 13 should not submit personal information or user content through Loop. If Loop learns that personal information from a child under 13 has been collected in a manner covered by applicable law, appropriate steps should be taken to delete it and address any required parental notice or consent.

Users between 13 and the age of legal majority should use Loop only with any permission required from a parent or legal guardian.

8. Security

Loop is being designed to minimize unnecessary collection and to use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information handled. No website, app, browser storage system, or network transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, especially during an early-access development phase.

9. Your choices

  • Decline or revoke browser location permission.
  • Clear Loop site data or browser storage to remove locally stored Alpha information.
  • Choose what profile details and community content you submit.
  • Stop using the Alpha at any time.

10. Changes to this policy

Loop may update this Privacy Policy as features, data practices, laws, or service providers change. The “Last updated” date will identify the current version. Material changes should be presented clearly before they take effect when required.

11. Contact and privacy requests

A dedicated legal/privacy email address has not yet been published for the Alpha. During Alpha testing, participants may use the in-app Send feedback form and select “Privacy or safety concern,” or use the same communication channel through which they received access to Loop. Dedicated operator identity and privacy contact information should be added before broader public release.
